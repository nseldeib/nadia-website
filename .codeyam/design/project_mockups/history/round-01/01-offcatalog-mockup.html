CEO and co-founder of CodeYam, where the work is memory, workflows, and simulations for AI-powered software development. The goal is to help more people build great software.

By Nadia Eldeib · CEO and co-founder of CodeYam

Software complexity is an old problem, and AI-generated code is accelerating it. CodeYam is the attempt to fix that. For people without an engineering background, it means building real applications instead of settling for generic templates. For engineers, it means designing and delivering meaningful products with far less friction.

Ships as a desktop extension for Claude, or runs in your terminal or IDE. The platform lives at codeyam.com, with a short demo and a blog on how it is built.

AI can help generate options, but humans still decide which ones are worth pursuing and why.
