The work

CodeYam

Software complexity is an old problem, and AI-generated code is accelerating it. CodeYam is the attempt to fix that. For people without an engineering background, it means building real applications instead of settling for generic templates. For engineers, it means designing and delivering meaningful products with far less friction.

Ships as a desktop extension for Claude, or runs in your terminal or IDE.

Live SimulationsAs AI writes your code, it simultaneously generates the data to run it, so you can see exactly how it behaves before you ship.
Design AssistanceTell CodeYam what you want to build, and it will offer a range of customizable design systems to choose from and to start building with.
Structured WorkflowsA rigorous 18-step process that walks the AI through building, testing, and simulating your code, step by step.
