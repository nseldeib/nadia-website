CEO & Co-founder

Why I’m building CodeYam

At Lyft I had a lightbulb moment: the thing we were building could touch millions of people’s lives every day. That set a bar I haven’t been able to unsee. CodeYam is the same idea one level down — if more people can build good software, more of the ideas worth having actually get made.

So we’re making it possible to build beautifully designed, highly maintainable software with AI. I co-founded it, I run it, and I’m still inside the product most weeks — which is the part I’d miss first if I ever stopped.

About

How I got here

Before CodeYam I worked across product and marketing at Lyft: the award-winning rider app redesign, and Bikes & Scooters from zero to millions of riders. Before that, product marketing at Kamcord, which Lyft acquired.

In 2021 I joined the inaugural South Park Commons Founder Fellowship — which is where I left Lyft and CodeYam began. I serve as Director of Growth on the board of Harvard Alumni in Tech. I studied at Harvard College, where I fenced NCAA Division I — which is where I learned that stubbornness, pointed somewhere useful, is a feature.
