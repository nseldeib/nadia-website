The work

CodeYam

Software complexity is an old problem, and AI-generated code is accelerating it. CodeYam is the attempt to fix that. For people without an engineering background, it means building real applications instead of settling for generic templates. For engineers, it means designing and delivering meaningful products with far less friction.

Ships as a desktop extension for Claude, or runs in your terminal or IDE. Live at codeyam.com, with a short demo and a blog.

Our goal is to help more people build great software. We believe the primary constraint to building something should be imagination, not technical expertise or tooling.
